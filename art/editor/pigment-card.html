A faded fresco puts skin and hair within 16 luma of each other, which quantises to mush. These two push them apart. Drop both to zero to see exactly what the painting said — and why it doesn't work at sprite size.

The limbs are stand-in capsules at the right proportions, there to show whether the palette holds together across body and face. The shaped sprites — hands, fists, sandalled feet, a draped himation — are the next piece of work.
